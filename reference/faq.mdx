## What is Coherence Atlas?

Coherence Atlas is Coherence Systems' developing product for investigating change in complex systems across time, scale, and state.

## What does “holds, drifts, and breaks” mean?

It frames questions about what persists, what gradually changes or degrades, and what may reorganize. These are research questions; the language does not establish a measured result for a particular system.

## Who is this work for?

Researchers, technical teams reviewing operational change, and potential partners exploring scoped evaluations. [Explore a potential fit](/guides/applied-pilots).

## Can I start using it today?

The public website describes an R&D-stage effort and exploratory collaboration. It does not announce general availability, self-service signup, pricing, or a public API. [Ask about your specific needs](/quickstart).

## Is the animation real customer data?

No. The website illustration uses synthetic data. It does not show customer data or measured performance.
