You do not need a complete proposal to start a conversation. A clear question and a little context are a useful beginning.

The [public website](https://coherencesystems.net) describes interest in research conversations, technical diligence, and exploratory applied pilots where the fit is appropriate.

## What would you like to understand?

Consider sharing:

- The kind of system or workflow you are studying
- The change you are trying to understand
- Why the question matters to your team
- What a useful evaluation would need to establish
- Any timing or practical constraints you want to discuss

For example: “We are trying to understand whether changing conditions in our system warrant closer review. We would like to discuss how to frame an evaluation.”

## What should we clarify together?

A first discussion can explore the question, comparison conditions, evidence needed, and limits on interpretation. Any proposed evaluation would need its own agreed scope.

Specific deliverables, access, schedules, and commercial terms are not offered by this guide. Bring those questions to the conversation.
