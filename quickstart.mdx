## What to include

- Your question
- Your organization or area of work, if relevant
- A short, non-confidential description of the context
- What you would like to discuss next

For a website question, include the page and the wording you would like clarified.

## Keep your first message high-level

Do not send confidential, proprietary, regulated, patient, medical, or other sensitive information through the public form. A submission does not create an NDA or a client relationship.

If a confidential discussion is needed, arrange a separate written agreement first. Read the [Legal Notice](https://coherencesystems.net/legal) and [Privacy Policy](https://coherencesystems.net/privacy).
