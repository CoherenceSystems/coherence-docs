Complex systems rarely announce when they begin to drift. Structure erodes gradually, state paths shift across basins, and by the time something is clearly wrong, the early signal has long since passed through the record unreviewed. Coherence Atlas gives you a principled way to watch that process in motion — keeping context visible, tracking whether meaningful structure persists, surfacing drift as it develops, and building precise language for transitions before they become crises.

## The Problem Coherence Atlas Solves

Conventional monitoring tools answer the question *"is something broken right now?"* Coherence Atlas answers a harder question: *"how has this system been changing, and does that change have structure?"* It does this by aligning three simultaneous views of every system you register — so you are never interpreting a signal in isolation.

## Four Capability Pillars

Every workflow in Coherence Atlas is organized around four capabilities. Each one addresses a distinct phase of how structural change is understood.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Context" icon="crosshairs">
    Keep system context, signals, conditions, and your review question visible before interpretation begins. Context is not a preamble — it is a constraint that shapes everything that follows.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Persistence" icon="hourglass-half">
    Study whether meaningful structure carries forward rather than appearing only momentarily regular. Persistence distinguishes signal from coincidence across the temporal record.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Drift" icon="arrows-left-right-to-line">
    Review gradual movement, divergence, and degradation within a stated context. Drift analysis gives you a longitudinal picture of how a system moves away from a reference state.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Transition" icon="shuffle">
    Build precise language for phase changes, regime shifts, and reorganizations that need deeper review. Transition detection surfaces the moments when a system stops drifting and begins to restructure.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Who Uses Coherence Atlas

Coherence Atlas is designed for technical teams working in signal-rich environments where understanding the *structure* of change matters as much as detecting its presence.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Researchers" icon="flask">
    Method development, simulations, and benchmark design. Use Coherence Atlas to evaluate whether analytical methods detect meaningful structure or surface noise as pattern.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Enterprise Teams" icon="building">
    Operational drift review across engineered and complex systems. Use Coherence Atlas to maintain structured, auditable records of how system behavior evolves over time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Partners" icon="handshake">
    Scoped evaluations and applied pilot workflows. Use Coherence Atlas to run structured assessments against defined review questions within a bounded context.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Application Domains

Coherence Atlas applies across any environment where signals are rich, time matters, and structural change has consequences:

- **Signal-rich research** — scientific instrumentation, experimental records, simulation outputs
- **Engineered and operational systems** — infrastructure, manufacturing, process control
- **Research instrumentation** — multi-sensor arrays, laboratory monitoring, benchmark suites
- **Structured review workflows** — compliance-adjacent analysis, longitudinal evaluation programs
